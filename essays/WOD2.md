My typing speed has increased considerbly since last time, and I can keep up with the WODs as long as I don't get distracted.
However, I still have problems with remembering to filter function input types as most of that is automatically handled in C#, something I take for granted.

Unit testing has alway been one of my weak points.  A number of my past works both in and out of the class have little to no unit tests to insure they work.
Most of my modded videogame works rely on a through intuition of the game it is made for, and have had their fair shares of bugs, although in my defense the mods are made for a game with a tiny community and poor code mod support.
To succeed in the next WODs, I will need to practice by ability to come up with suitable unit tests for every sitsuation in a timely manner.
